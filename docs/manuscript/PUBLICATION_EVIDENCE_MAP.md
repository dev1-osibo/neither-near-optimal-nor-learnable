# Publication evidence map

This map connects each reader-facing study stage to its scientific purpose and claim boundary.

| Study stage | Why it was included | Evidence reported | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corrected primary controller comparison | Re-evaluate earlier controller claims with causal normalization, an environment-consistent hindsight reference, service qualification, paired weeks, and common terminal accounting. | 240 learned policies; 136 individually eligible; 4 of 48 algorithm–configuration slots eligible across all five seeds. | Corrected 2024–2025 reanalysis, not pristine prospective confirmation; the incomplete family supports no primary Holm-corrected cost-superiority claim. |
| Supplemental accounting replay | Check foresight, objective-weight, and sizing records against frozen environment and terminal channels instead of trusting serialized summaries. | 209 records replayed over all 103 weeks and 3,616,536 record-hours, with zero maximum residual across 27 audited channels. | Verifies recorded replay arithmetic; it does not establish physical deployment validity or make service-violating policies eligible. |
| Fixed-policy causal price-input ablation | Test whether changing only the causal price summary changes one fixed threshold controller. | Seasonal-naive weekly price summaries recovered 7.414% of the opportunity-weighted persistence-to-hindsight gap; both arms retained zero modeled service violations. | Descriptive and post-primary; not MPC, not a general forecast claim, and not real-world savings evidence. |
| History-conditioned recipe comparison | Test a predeclared recurrent PPO recipe beside a matched feedforward PPO training recipe in storage-rich configurations. | 40 policies across four configurations; all four recurrent slots failed the all-five-seed service screen. | Exploratory; unequal parameter counts and finite seeds prevent attribution to memory alone. |
| Exact training-budget continuation | Test whether conclusions change at fixed two-million and five-million interaction snapshots from the same trajectories. | 60 policies; across 12 primary transitions there was one recovery, nine persistent failures, and two regressions. | Exploratory and not a convergence test; no budget or checkpoint was selected from outcomes. |
| Reserved prospective confirmation | Preserve a separate later interval for a future predeclared evaluation. | No result is reported for `[2026-01-05T00:00:00Z, 2027-01-04T00:00:00Z)`. | The current release is not prospective confirmation. |

## Verification anchors

- Primary and supplemental conclusions were independently reproduced from the complete preserved evidence populations.
- The fixed-policy price-input result was replayed across all 2,472 controller/configuration episodes and 415,296 environment steps; it is reported descriptively and not labeled MPC.
- The history-conditioning and training-budget conclusions were independently reproduced from the complete 100-policy evaluation under the declared analysis rules.
- Figure inputs retain source-population digests in [publication_results.json](figures/publication_results.json); the renderer checks the complete compact dataset before producing any figure.
