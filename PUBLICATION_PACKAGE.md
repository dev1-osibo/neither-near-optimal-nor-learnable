# Publication package

This is the public companion to the Data-Centre Energy-Control Claim Qualification Benchmark article.

Included:

- the complete preprint and non-technical overview;
- eight article figures in PNG and SVG;
- the compact result dataset used by those figures;
- a digest-gated renderer; and
- public validation tests for population completeness, reported totals, metadata, and package hygiene.

Not included:

- raw workload or energy datasets;
- trained policy files;
- research-operation records, review prompts, custody material, or temporary execution captures; and
- private development history that is unnecessary for understanding or reproducing the publication figures.

The package does not rerun controller training or the simulated environments. It reproduces the published visualizations from the complete compact weekly populations and checks their declared counts. The 2024–2025 evidence is a retrospective reanalysis, and the reserved 2026 interval has not yet been reported.
